Here's how it comes together.
<break time="1.5s" />
First, video and telemetry are aligned, and sharp, overlapping frames are selected.
<break time="1.5s" />
Light Glue connects matching features across those frames. Our bundle adjustment solver then refines the cameras and lens parameters using image evidence and G P S.
<break time="1.5s" />
Next, our G P U stereo implementation estimates depth. Neighbouring views cross check those estimates before Open 3D fuses them into a textured surface.
<break time="1.5s" />
The pipeline reports progress and previews to the desktop viewer while it runs. Geo-referencing connects the finished model to real-world coordinates.
<break time="1.5s" />
Now let's use it!
<break time="1.5s" />
Switch to confidence colours to inspect reliability. Click across a road to measure distance. Outline a roof for its area. Check a volume, or trace an elevation profile.
<break time="1.5s" />
Then take those measurements into your G I S workflow with Geo JSON or K M L, alongside the exported models and maps.
<break time="1.5s" />
Our recorded reconstruction contains around eight million vertices. On a separate Spain benchmark, thirteen stable features showed twenty-nine centimetre median horizontal agreement with the national reference orthophoto.
<break time="1.5s" />
Our unique value brings these pieces together: our own reconstruction core, visible confidence on every surface, and practical measurement tools, from one flight to usable results.
