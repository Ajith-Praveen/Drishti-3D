One drone flight. Hundreds of rooftops. A whole neighbourhood of possibilities.
<break time="1.5s" />
Now imagine being able to measure that neighbourhood: the width of a road, the area of a roof, even the volume of a stockpile. All from the video you just captured.
<break time="1.5s" />
Meet Drishti 3D!
<break time="1.5s" />
Built for N T R O's single-pass reconstruction challenge, Drishti turns drone video and flight data into a geo-referenced 3D model.
<break time="1.5s" />
Here's our working M V P. Load the video, add the flight log, and hit Run! The application selects useful frames, solves the camera positions, and builds the model as you watch.
<break time="1.5s" />
Then you can explore it, measure it, and export the results, all inside the same desktop application.
<break time="1.5s" />
The exciting part is the engine we built underneath.
<break time="1.5s" />
We developed our own camera optimisation solver and G P U stereo implementation, designed around the challenges of a single flight path.
<break time="1.5s" />
Our custom solver made the camera optimisation steps around thirteen times faster than our earlier implementation on the benchmark flight.
<break time="1.5s" />
We also tested against Col Map, the standard research tool, on the same laptop. Col Map took thirty-three minutes for a sparse model. Drishti delivered a dense, textured, geo-referenced model in under ten minutes.
<break time="1.5s" />
And where the camera position is exact, our surface landed almost a metre closer to the surveyed points than Col Map's.
<break time="1.5s" />
That's the engineering behind our promise: turn captured footage into usable geometry, with evidence for what the model has measured.
