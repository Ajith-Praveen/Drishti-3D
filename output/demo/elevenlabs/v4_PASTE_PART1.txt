One drone. One pass. One ordinary video. What if that was all you needed for a measurable 3D map of the ground? Meet Drishti 3D, from team Robos dot Inc.
<break time="1.0s" />
Problem statement S I H two six one five eight asks for a geo-referenced 3D model from one pass of drone video and G P S, accurate to a metre, in under fifteen minutes for every ten minutes of video.
<break time="1.0s" />
Here is the real app. I load an eleven minute D J I flight and its G P S log, and press Run. The model builds live, keyframe by keyframe, so the operator sees a bad pass while the drone is still in the air, and can fly it again right away.
<break time="1.0s" />
Under the hood: smart keyframe selection, our own camera solver locked to G P S, and stereo depth from every camera, kept only where other cameras agree. Measured, not guessed.
<break time="1.0s" />
And Drishti is honest about it. Every part of the surface is tagged measured, low confidence or inferred, and ground that no camera saw is left open, never invented.
<break time="1.0s" />
Then measure: coordinates, distances, areas, volumes and elevation profiles, exported straight to G I S. And our new side by side view puts the drone's own video frame next to the model, so anyone can check the geometry with their own eyes.
