 1. This is Drishti 3D.
 2. It turns a single drone flight into a measured, geo-referenced 3D model.
 3. The challenge from N T R O: from one pass of drone video, build a 3D model accurate to a metre, in under fifteen minutes per ten minutes of video.
 4. Our input is an ordinary drone video, here an eleven minute flight over a town, with the drone's G P S log.
 5. In our desktop app, load the video and the flight log, and press Run.
 6. Each stage runs on its own.
 7. It picks sharp keyframes, matches features between them, and solves where every camera was.
 8. Then depth is measured, view by view, and the model builds up, live on screen.
 9. Under the hood, Light Glue matches features on the G P U, and our own bundle adjustment solves the cameras, and calibrates the lens.
10. Plane sweep stereo measures depth, cross view checks keep only confirmed depth, and it is all fused into one mesh.
11. So it is real 3D, not a height map.
12. Trees and houses keep their true shape, instead of turning into prisms.
13. The result is a textured model of about eight million points, and over eighty percent of it is directly measured.
14. Every surface is colour coded by confidence.
15. Green is measured by two or more cameras, yellow is low confidence, and red is inferred.
16. Measurements snap to the surface.
17. Point coordinates, distance, roof area, volume, and elevation profiles, ready to export to Geo JSON or K M L.
18. Here, a roof of about eight hundred and fifty square metres, and a hundred metre stretch of road.
19. Each run also writes standard files.
20. O B J, P L Y, L A S, G L T F and F B X models, and Geo TIFF maps, like this true orthomosaic.
21. On a public benchmark flight in Spain, median error is under thirty centimetres against the national I G N reference.
22. And our eleven minute flight is processed in about seven minutes, on one laptop.
23. It is built with Python, PyTorch on Apple Metal or NVIDIA CUDA, Open C V, Open 3D, and a Q T and V T K desktop app.
24. It is covered by seven hundred and thirty nine automated tests.
25. Drishti 3D. One flight, a 3D model you can measure.
