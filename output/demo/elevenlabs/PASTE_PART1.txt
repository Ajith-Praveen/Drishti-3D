This is Drishti 3D.
<break time="1.5s" />
It turns a single drone flight into a measured, geo-referenced 3D model.
<break time="1.5s" />
The challenge from N T R O: from one pass of drone video, build a 3D model accurate to a metre, in under fifteen minutes per ten minutes of video.
<break time="1.5s" />
Our input is an ordinary drone video, here an eleven minute flight over a town, with the drone's G P S log.
<break time="1.5s" />
In our desktop app, load the video and the flight log, and press Run.
<break time="1.5s" />
Each stage runs on its own.
<break time="1.5s" />
It picks sharp keyframes, matches features between them, and solves where every camera was.
<break time="1.5s" />
Then depth is measured, view by view, and the model builds up, live on screen.
<break time="1.5s" />
Under the hood, Light Glue matches features on the G P U, and our own bundle adjustment solves the cameras, and calibrates the lens.
<break time="1.5s" />
Plane sweep stereo measures depth, cross view checks keep only confirmed depth, and it is all fused into one mesh.
<break time="1.5s" />
So it is real 3D, not a height map.
<break time="1.5s" />
Trees and houses keep their true shape, instead of turning into prisms.
<break time="1.5s" />
The result is a textured model of about eight million points, and over eighty percent of it is directly measured.
